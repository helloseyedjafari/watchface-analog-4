# Watch face editors and customization experiences across smartwatch platforms (benchmark for a "build your own face" experience on Wear OS)

State as of 2026-10-08. Evidence-quality note: most facts below were gathered via web search summaries plus fetches of primary pages (developer.android.com, developer.samsung.com, Android Developers Blog). Where a claim rests only on a search-result summary (the underlying page was not opened by me) it is tagged **[snippet-only]**. Where the source is older than ~2024 it is tagged **[older]**. Claims I could not source are in the Gaps sections.

## 1. Wear OS / Galaxy Watch: what system editors, authoring tools and phone-side editors offer

### Takeaway
On Wear OS the system editor is face-defined, not free-form: a face author pre-declares the colour themes, list options, booleans and complication slots, and the user can only pick among them (no hex input, no slider, no moving elements, in anything I could find). Free-form building exists only on the third-party side (Facer, WatchMaker, Pujie) and in creator tools (Watch Face Studio, Watch Face Designer, Facer Creator), and the platform is narrowing the third-party side by forcing everything into the declarative Watch Face Format (WFF).

### Cited Findings

**Watch Face Format (WFF) - what an end user can be offered in the Wear OS system editor**
- WFF user configuration has three types: `BooleanConfiguration` (show/hide or two styles), `ListConfiguration` (e.g. several background images), and `ColorConfiguration` (colour *themes*; each `ColorOption` is a fixed set of hex colours the author hard-codes, referenced as `[CONFIGURATION.myThemeColor.1]`). The page does not document any numeric/slider or free colour-picker configuration. — [Define user configurations](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/wff/personalization/user-configurations)
- `Flavors` (WFF v2+) bundle preset values of several configurations (and complication settings) and are selectable from the companion app; users can also set each configuration individually. The page states the number of combinations can grow overwhelmingly, which is why flavors exist. — [Define user configurations](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/wff/personalization/user-configurations)
- WFF release notes: v2 added Flavors, goal-progress and weighted-elements complication types, weather, heart rate; v3 added auto-sizing text and blend modes; **v4 added user-selected photos**, ambient-mode transitions and colour transformations/tinting; v5 added blend mode on Group/ComplicationSlot, line spacing on Text, TextCircular auto-size, `join` on strokes, and nested settings / dynamic complications on `ListOption`. Numeric configurations are not mentioned. — [WFF release notes](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/wff/release-notes)
- WFF v4 Photos element: a `PhotosConfiguration` (id, `configType` e.g. SINGLE) plus a `Photos` element inside a `PartImage`; supports a single photo or a gallery, advance automatically or on tap; the user picks photos on the phone companion app. **[snippet-only]** — [What's new in watch faces (Android Developers Blog, May 2025)](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2025/05/whats-new-in-watch-faces.html)
- A `ListOption` in v5 supports nested settings (a second-level option under a first-level choice). Release-notes text only; no UI detail found. — [WFF release notes](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/wff/release-notes)
- Legacy (non-WFF) faces: since 14 Jan 2026 they can no longer be installed from Google Play; installed ones keep working but cannot be updated. **[snippet-only]** — [9to5Google, Wear OS legacy watch face](https://9to5google.com/2025/06/12/wear-os-legacy-watch-face/) (as summarised by search; the date claim I did not open the page to verify)
- Pixel Watch system editor: press and hold, tap Edit, swipe left to scroll through styles and complication layouts; Pixel Watch app on the phone can also edit colours and complications. — [Google Pixel Watch Help: Customize your watch face and background](https://support.google.com/googlepixelwatch/answer/12662644?hl=en)
- Android Central describes the crown cycling through fixed choices (colours, indicators, etc.) in that editor. **[older, written for Wear OS 3]** — [Android Central: How to customize watch faces on Pixel Watch](https://androidcentral.com/wearables/wear-os/how-to-change-pixel-watch-complications)
- Pixel "Photos" face: up to 30 photos from the user's album, added from the Pixel Watch app or the watch; requires Pixel Watch app and Google Photos on phone and watch. Search found no way to put a photo behind other Google faces. — [Google Pixel Watch Help](https://support.google.com/googlepixelwatch/answer/12662644?hl=en) and [Google Photos Help](https://support.google.com/photos/answer/12776450?hl=en-GB) (the "no photo behind other faces" statement is my reading of an absent result, not a source statement)
- Wear OS 6 Material You theming: an accent is applied across apps; Settings > Display > Color theme offers eight options, defaulting to the face's colours. **[snippet-only]** — [9to5Google, Wear OS 6 preview](https://9to5google.com/2025/05/20/wear-os-6-preview-gallery/)
- Pixel Watch complication bounding behaviour: Pixel Watch 3 reportedly always shows "Icon + Text + Title" layout and ignores custom text-field widths (developer forum report). **[older, 2023-24, developer forum]** — [Samsung forum: Pixel Watch not honoring complication layout settings](https://forum.developer.samsung.com/t/pixel-watch-not-honoring-complication-layout-settings/23353)

**Samsung / Galaxy Watch**
- Galaxy Wearable on the phone: Watch faces > pick face > pencil icon; per-face you can choose pre-made customizations or background, numeral/hand colours and complications; Samsung states not all faces support them. — [Samsung support: Customize your Galaxy Watch face](https://www.samsung.com/ae/support/mobile-devices/how-do-i-customise-the-watch-face-on-my-galaxy-watch/)
- Samsung's older "My style / Graphical" face: Customise > Background > Camera or Gallery, then builds a colour palette from the photo so you can drag the picture onto a palette. **[older, snippet-only]** — [Samsung support](https://www.samsung.com/ae/support/mobile-devices/how-do-i-customise-the-watch-face-on-my-galaxy-watch/)
- **Watch Face Studio (WFS) Photo slot (April 2026 blog):** one Photo slot per project; user opens Galaxy Wearable > Customize > "+" to add phone photos, deletable there, first image becomes background; designer chooses "When watch face is tapped" (default) or "When watch wakes". Targets Galaxy Watches running Wear OS powered by Samsung; no min versions stated. — [Samsung Developers: Personalized Watch Faces with Photo Slot](https://developer.samsung.com/watch-face-studio/blog/en/2026/04/28/personalized-watch-faces-with-photo-slot)
- WFS complications: user-editable vs "fixed" type per slot; Samsung staff said complications not following theme colours on some watches is by design (suggested filing a feature request). **[older, 2022-24 forum]** — [Samsung forum: Complications ignore theme colors](https://forum.developer.samsung.com/t/complications-ignore-theme-colors-on-watch-5-pro/27638/2); [Samsung codelab: edge complication slots](https://developer.samsung.com/codelab/watch-face-studio/edge-complication-digital-clock.html)
- WFS slot-shape types: circle, line, small box, large box, edge. **[snippet-only]** — [Samsung Developers blog: Design Complications Using WFS](https://developer.samsung.com/sdp/blog/en/2022/08/17/design-complications-using-watch-face-studio)
- Samsung community feature request: let users choose colours for each part of the face and complications, indicating this is not offered per-element on stock faces. **[snippet-only, one user post]** — [Samsung Community feature request](https://us.community.samsung.com/t5/Galaxy-Watch/Feature-Request-Please-allow-watch-faces-more-customization-by/m-p/2954264/highlight/true)

**Google Watch Face Designer (Figma plugin, Aug 2025)**
- Figma plugin, targets Wear OS 4+, supports digital and analog time, interactive and ambient mode, custom fonts, complications, and automatic theme creation from Figma styles; export directly to Play Store, to Android Studio or Watch Face Studio, or preview on a real device. It is a *designer* tool, not an end-user editor. — [Android Developers Blog: Introducing Watch Face Designer](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2025/08/introducing-watch-face-designer.html)
- Complication slots are Figma frames with Behavior "Complication slot"; slot settings Default app and Always-on; user chooses which app goes in which slot; complication text cannot use a custom font (always the system font, typically Roboto). Colour themes are Figma styles named `Face/Element Family/Colour name/Element`, and users pick a theme from the watch face picker (example: 2 hand options x 3 dial options mixed and matched). — [Advanced usage of Watch Face Designer](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-designer/advanced)
- I found no 2026-specific news on Watch Face Designer changes (search turned up nothing newer than the Aug 2025 post). — [search of Android Developers Blog; none found]

**Third-party creator platforms and phone-side editors**
- Facer Creator: web editor (facer.io/creator, desktop recommended); supports WFF for publishing to Wear OS 6+; "Rosetta Stone" publishes once to Apple Watch and Wear OS; blank/analog/digital canvas, image import, text/date/complications, ambient mode editing. **[snippet-only for features; announcement is Facer's own]** — [Facer: A New Era for Watch Face Design](https://news.facer.io/a-new-era-for-watch-face-design-welcome-to-the-future-of-facer-creator/); [Android Central: How to create custom watch faces on Wear OS](https://www.androidcentral.com/how-create-custom-watch-faces-wear-os) **[older]**
- Facer Wear OS 6 update (24 Jul 2025): "Power Impact" feature lets users choose visuals vs battery trade-off; "Looks" lets users share custom styles; Facer Premium first year $14.99. — [Facer news: Massive update](https://news.facer.io/massive-facer-update-wear-os-6-new-features-for-all/)
- Facer, Pujie and WatchMaker moved to WFF for Wear OS 6; press notes WFF limits 3D/depth/shadow effects and some of the "extreme customization" the old platforms were known for (Tecnoandroid's conclusion). **[snippet-only]** — [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/after-facer-pujie-and-watchmaker-announce-wear-os-6-support-3581417/); [Tecnoandroid](https://www.tecnoandroid.it/2025/08/03/dopo-facer-anche-pujie-e-watchmaker-supportano-wear-os-6-1602082/)
- WatchMaker store listing claims a from-scratch designer with animations, complications, video backgrounds, tap actions; customizes colours, layouts, hands, fonts, backgrounds. Developer-written listing, not independent. **[snippet-only]**. Gizmodo (old) said import of images was behind a one-time in-app purchase of $2.99 **[older]**. — [WatchMaker on APKMirror](https://apkmirror.com/apk/androidslide/watchmaker-watch-faces); [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/design-your-own-android-wear-watch-faces-no-coding-req-1748087304)
- Pujie Black (phone-side editor sending to the watch): tabs for analog/digital clock, calendar, indicators, background, tick marks and numbers; preview before export; a "Colors Scheduler" that changes colours by time of day; $2 on Android with paid cloud library. **[older, 2015-2019 sources; pre-WFF]** — [Android Central: Pujie Black](https://www.androidcentral.com/get-style-you-want-pujie-black-watch-face); [Droid Life](https://www.droid-life.com/2019/07/10/pujie-black-available-for-samsung-galaxy-wearables/)
- **Watch Face Push API (Wear OS 6+):** lets an app add/update/remove watch faces directly; faces must be WFF; marketplace apps are the canonical use case; Facer, TIMEFLIK, WatchMaker, Pujie and Recreative helped design it. Google lists "apps that let users design and customize watch faces" as a higher-complexity use and recommends a watch + phone app. **[snippet-only]** — [Watch Face Push (developer.android.com)](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-push); [Android Developers Blog, May 2025](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2025/05/whats-new-in-watch-faces.html)
- Answers to the specific feature questions, as best sourced: free hex colours - not in WFF `ColorConfiguration` docs (fixed option sets); sliders - not documented; arbitrary complication placement by end user - not found (slots are fixed by author; Samsung says edge slots' "placement, size or rotation angle" cannot be changed); per-complication backgrounds - not found as an end-user option; user photo backgrounds - yes (WFF v4 Photos; Samsung Photo slot; Pixel Photos face); onboarding flows - none documented; the closest is Flavors presets and the Pixel face picker.

### Inferences
- The Wear OS ceiling for end-user customization is "pick among author-defined options"; anything like a hex picker, numeric slider, or movable elements would have to be implemented by an author app (phone-side editor + Watch Face Push), generating a WFF package, rather than by the platform editor. This matches the design direction of the project's own builder-app spec.
- Photo support is the one "free" input the platform now gives users (WFF v4 / Samsung Photo slot), but it is a background-only, single-slot feature on both platforms.
- Because WFF is declarative, the "number of combinations explodes" problem (Google's own Flavors rationale) applies to any editor offering free colour + numeric controls; presets/Flavors-like starting points are the platform-sanctioned answer.

### Gaps
- No independent hands-on review of Watch Face Designer, WatchMaker's current WFF editor, or Pujie's post-WFF editor was found; claims about their editors rest on listings and announcements.
- Whether any WFF release after v5 adds numeric/slider configurations: not found in the release notes (they list only v2-v5).
- Whether Facer or WatchMaker expose hex colour entry or sliders in their current WFF in-app customization: not verified.
- Galaxy Watch 8 / One UI 8 Watch stock editor details (palette sizes, per-complication colour) not verified from a primary source.

## 2. Other platforms as benchmarks (Garmin, Fitbit/Pixel, Apple, Pebble/Rebble, Amazfit, Xiaomi, Huawei)

### Takeaway
Apple and Garmin treat the editor as a per-face system of fixed options with a Photos face as the headline free-form feature; Huawei and Xiaomi offer a photo ("Gallery"/"Custom") face with style/colour/layout choices in the phone app; Pebble's Clay gives developers a real colour-picker and toggles on a phone settings page. Reviewers praise Apple's breadth and Photos face, and criticise Wear OS/Pixel on variety.

### Cited Findings
- **Apple Watch:** Press and hold, Edit, swipe through per-face pages (colour, style, dial, complications) with the Digital Crown; also editable in the iPhone Watch app. Complication slots vary per face (Flow, Artist, Flux have none). **[snippet-only]** — [the5krunner watchOS 26 faces guide](https://the5krunner.com/2025/11/03/watchos-26-apple-watch-faces-guide/); [Apple support: faces and features](https://support.apple.com/en-kg/guide/watch/apde9218b440/6.0/watchos)
- Apple Photos face (watchOS 26): redesigned with Liquid Glass so the time adapts translucently; sync an album or specific people, pets, places; reviewers called it the most positively received. **[snippet-only]** — [Tom's Guide on watchOS 26 faces](https://www.tomsguide.com/wellness/smartwatches/watchos-26-arrives-today-i-tried-the-new-apple-watch-faces-and-theres-a-clear-winner); [BGR](https://bgr.com/1964862/watchos-26-new-watch-faces-hands-on-images/)
- Apple Waypoint (Ultra): Tom's Guide's pick of the new faces; four customizable complications including the big circle that can be moved left/right or removed. Flow criticised for having no complications. **[snippet-only]** — [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/wellness/smartwatches/watchos-26-arrives-today-i-tried-the-new-apple-watch-faces-and-theres-a-clear-winner)
- Apple Watch Face Gallery categories (Health & Fitness, Photos, Data Rich). **[snippet-only]** — [iGeeksBlog](https://www.igeeksblog.com/best-apple-watch-faces/)
- How-To Geek: complication caps vary (some faces none, Infograph up to eight); Infograph "a nightmare to read at a glance". **[snippet-only]** — [How-To Geek](https://www.howtogeek.com/698375/how-to-make-the-most-of-complications-on-your-apple-watch/)
- Third-party Apple Watch complication/photo apps (WatchAnything $1.99, 3.9 stars from 87 ratings per listing; Chronomizer) exist because stock faces limit photo complications. **[snippet-only]** — [WatchAnything on App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1461710896); [Chronomizer](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1575316929)
- **Garmin:** On the watch, Customize offers number style, hand style, data fields, accent colour, background colour, data colour (options vary per face). — [Garmin manual: Watch Face Settings](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-676967A0-1B23-4384-9BC9-76F3D643F1C8/EN-US/GUID-A4EA51C3-D0B2-48E3-807A-768C6F07D7BA.html)

- Garmin Connect IQ faces expose developer-defined settings in the phone app (colours, data fields, fonts, offsets) per one third-party developer's docs; no official Garmin page confirming that exact path was found. — [watchface.io docs](https://watchface.io/docs/edit-settings)
- **Pebble / Rebble:** Clay generates a phone-side config page from JSON with Color Picker, Toggle, Select, Radio Group, Checkbox Group and Input; example faces expose ring colour, background colour, seconds hand colour, toggles for hands/dots. Limitation: settings only apply on submit, no live preview (forum request for instant update). — [Rebble developer docs: app configuration](https://developer.rebble.io/guides/user-interfaces/app-configuration/); [Rebble forum: Clay](https://forum.rebble.io/t/rebble-clay-new-way-to-configure-your-pebble-and-core-apps-and-faces/331?page=2); [Color Circle (GitHub)](https://github.com/zackdolsen/ColorCircle)

Complications in the Zepp maker: not verified.
- **Huawei:** Huawei Health > Watch faces > Mine > Gallery: add photos (camera/gallery), pick Style and Layout, set Widgets, multiple photos switchable by tap; adjust image display area. ### Inferences
- Across every platform, the mainstream native editor is fixed options per face; true free-form building lives in creator apps or developer-defined settings pages (Clay, Connect IQ). A phone-side editor with a real colour picker is a recognised pattern (Pebble Clay) whose known weakness is no live preview.
- The photo background is the single cross-platform "free" feature (Apple, Garmin Face It, Huawei, Xiaomi, Samsung, Pixel, WFF v4).

### Gaps
- No primary source on Fitbit-specific (Ionic/Versa-era) clock face editor behaviour; not searched in depth.
- Amazfit Zepp phone-side editing (non-developer) and complication behaviour not verified.
- No official Garmin source for the Connect IQ per-face settings screen; Pebble/Rebble Time 2 status not verified beyond developer docs.
- Reviewer praise on Garmin/Huawei/Xiaomi customization not found.

## 3. Ring / arc complications: full ring, halves, thirds, quarters, styling

### Takeaway
Arc/edge complications exist in all major authoring routes, but end-user choice of ring *partitioning* (full / 2 / 3 / 4) was found nowhere; partition counts are fixed by the face author (Samsung Duo or Quadrant; Pixel Track "Double"). I found no example of a face or editor with 3 thirds, and no editor where the user picks the split.

### Cited Findings
- Samsung WFS edge slots come in two types, **Duo** (two complications, left and right) and **Quadrant** (four complications); only one type per project; placement, size and rotation cannot be changed, but unused slots can be hidden or deleted. — [Samsung codelab: edge complication slots](https://developer.samsung.com/codelab/watch-face-studio/edge-complication-digital-clock.html)
- The same codelab shows a ranged-value complication rendered as a progress bar ("Progress bar + Icon + Text"); it does not describe a ring. — [Samsung codelab](https://developer.samsung.com/codelab/watch-face-studio/edge-complication-digital-clock.html)
- WFS forum: circular progress bar can be set with ranges 0-180 or 180-360 to make a half-arc; the slot boundary stays rectangular/circular; staff said a freeform arc is achievable in Android Studio but not WFS (forum answers conflicted). **[older, forum]** — [Samsung forum: How to add arc complications?](https://forum.developer.samsung.com/t/how-to-add-arc-complications/28832)
- Edge-slot Duo/Quadrant complications failed to appear on Pixel Watch 3 in one 2024 report, said to be device-specific pending Wear OS 5.1. **[older, forum]** — [Samsung forum: Complication problems (Pixel Watch 3)](https://forum.developer.samsung.com/t/complication-problems-pixel-watch-3-1-8-7/39132)
- Google Wear OS complication docs: arcs along the edge are a standard shape; RANGED_VALUE and GOAL_PROGRESS suit edge arcs; Wear OS 4 added complication bounding areas (content outside is cropped). **[snippet-only]** — [Provide useful data through complications](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/wff/complications)
- Pixel Watch 3 **Track** face: the ring for a metric is the defining feature; layouts Single, Bulb, and **Double, which splits the ring in half with two complications above the time**; rings are thin on Pixel Watch 3. — [9to5Google: Pixel Watch 3 new faces](https://9to5google.com/2024/09/13/pixel-watch-3-new-faces/)
- Pixel Watch 3 **Active** face "Plus" adds a ring of four complications; **Field** "Arcs" has four radial complications between two sets of indices. — [9to5Google: Pixel Watch 3 new faces](https://9to5google.com/2024/09/13/pixel-watch-3-new-faces/)
- Pixel Watch 4 faces reportedly support as many as 8 complications (e.g. 4 arc-style around the edge, 4 circular in the centre). **[snippet-only, unverified]** — [9to5Google/Android Authority via search](https://9to5google.com/2024/11/29/pixel-watch-concentric-face/)
- Reviewer caution: on Pixel Watch 4, complications placed around the glass circumference can be hard to see due to glare. — [GSMArena: Pixel Watch 4 hands-on](https://m.gsmarena.com/google_pixel_watch_4_handson_review-news-70601.php) **[snippet-only]**
- WFF v2 added "goal progress" and "weighted elements" complication types, the building blocks for ring and segmented-ring renderings. — [WFF release notes](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/wff/release-notes)

### Inferences
- A user-selectable ring partition (1/2/3/4 segments) with styling is not found in any mainstream editor reviewed; that would be a differentiator, but the evidence is absence-of-results, not a survey of all faces on Facer/WatchMaker (which have 100k+ faces and could contain examples I did not find).
- Duo = halves and Quadrant = quarters are the existing vocabulary on Samsung; "thirds" has no precedent in what I found.

### Gaps
- No example of a three-segment ring complication found.
- No source on styling options (stroke width, gaps, caps, per-segment colour) in any user-facing editor; only the v5 `join` stroke attribute for authors.
- Facer/WatchMaker catalogue was not searched for ring-partition examples.

## 4. Evidence on which customization features drive downloads, retention and paid conversion

### Takeaway
I found no rigorous data (surveys, retention or conversion studies) tying specific customization features to downloads or paid conversion. ### Inferences
- The strongest (still indirect) signals are: user photos, complications, and colour variety are what reviewers and platform owners emphasise; paid conversion historically attached to editor power (image import, creator tools) or catalogue access (Facer Premium), not to colour pickers in particular.
- The uncited claim circulating in how-to sites that customization raises satisfaction was flagged by that source's own search summary as unverified; do not use it.

### Gaps
- No Play Store review analysis (e.g. frequency of "colour", "complication", "photo" in reviews of Facer, WatchMaker, Pujie, Pixel Watch Faces) was done.
- No retention or conversion data by feature; no download figures for 2025-26 (Facer's figures are from 2018).
- No Reddit threads were retrievable via the search tool; user-voice evidence is thin (a few forum feature requests).

## 5. Features nobody offers on Wear OS (from the evidence above, with confidence)

### Takeaway
By the sources reviewed, these are not offered in the Wear OS/Galaxy platform editors: free hex colour input, numeric sliders, end-user moving of elements, user-chosen ring partitioning, per-complication backgrounds, and a guided onboarding builder. They are absence-of-evidence findings and should be validated by hands-on testing before being claimed in marketing.

### Cited Findings
- WFF user configuration offers only Boolean, List and Color (fixed-theme) types, plus Flavors and (v4) Photos; no numeric/slider or free colour type documented. — [Define user configurations](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/wff/personalization/user-configurations); [WFF release notes](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/wff/release-notes)
- Complication slots are author-fixed; Samsung edge slots cannot be moved/resized/rotated. — [Samsung codelab](https://developer.samsung.com/codelab/watch-face-studio/edge-complication-digital-clock.html)
- Complication text is always drawn in the system font in Watch Face Designer faces. — [Advanced usage of Watch Face Designer](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-designer/advanced)
- Samsung user feature request for per-element colour choice suggests it is not offered on stock faces. **[snippet-only]** — [Samsung Community](https://us.community.samsung.com/t5/Galaxy-Watch/Feature-Request-Please-allow-watch-faces-more-customization-by/m-p/2954264/highlight/true)
- Watch Face Push is explicitly available for design-and-customize apps, which is the platform route for a phone-side builder (Wear OS 6+). **[snippet-only]** — [Watch Face Push](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-push)

### Inferences
- Benchmarks outside Wear OS that a best-in-class builder can borrow: Apple's per-face Edit pages with Photos face and Digital Crown browsing; Garmin Face It's photo + template + data flow; Pebble Clay's colour picker + toggles (add live preview, its known gap); Huawei/Xiaomi photo-with-style flow; Samsung's palette extracted from a chosen photo; Pujie's colour scheduler by time of day; Facer's "Looks" sharing and "Power Impact" battery trade-off.

### Gaps
- No hands-on testing of the current Facer, WatchMaker, Pujie or Galaxy Watch editors was done, so presence of hex/slider controls there is unconfirmed.
