## Who is actually using Watch Face Push (Facer, TIMEFLIK, WatchMaker, Pujie, Recreative) and does anyone ship it publicly?

### Takeaway
Google named Facer, TIMEFLIK, WatchMaker, Pujie and Recreative as design partners in May 2025. Facer, Pujie and TIMEFLIK each have a shipped Wear OS 6 app; Facer is confirmed to use the push mechanism (a single-slot "watch app that delivers the face file"). WatchMaker and Recreative have no confirmed public Push-based marketplace in what I could verify. Google itself ships a non-marketplace example (Androidify, Dec 2025) built on the same API.

### Cited Findings
- The Watch Face Push API was announced in May 2025 for developers building their own watch face marketplaces. It requires Wear OS 6+ and works only with Watch Face Format (WFF) faces. Facer, TIMEFLIK, WatchMaker, Pujie and Recreative "helped design" it. Facer, Recreative and TIMEFLIK are shown developing marketplace apps for Wear OS 6. — [Android Developers Blog, What's new in Watch Faces (May 2025)](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2025/05/whats-new-in-watch-faces.html)
- Facer's Wear OS 6 app was reported ready for the Galaxy Watch 8 launch (article dated 2025-07-24). Facer had lost compatibility with Wear OS 5 watches about a year earlier. — [9to5Google, Facer Wear OS 6 launch](https://9to5google.com/2025/07/24/facer-wear-os-6-launch/) (this article itself does not mention Watch Face Push; the link between Facer and Push comes from the next items)
- Facer's own community beta notes (announced 2025-07-21): the phone app installs a new Facer watch app on Wear OS 6 watches (Galaxy Watch 8 selectable); users must open the Facer watch app and "Allow Facer" via the Wear OS system prompt so it can set watch faces. — [Facer community: Wear OS 6 beta overview](https://community.facer.io/t/facer-wear-os-6-beta-testing-overview-release-notes/97851)
- Facer community users describe the Wear OS 6 behavior as: the Facer watch app stores only one face (single slot), no favorites list, faces sit alongside Play Store faces, and the watch app "only delivers the watch face file." — [Facer community thread, Aug 2025](https://community.facer.io/t/sync-issues-unpublished-faces-won-t-transfer-to-galaxy-watch-8-classic/101165) (user forum posts, not official statements)
- Press coverage says Facer integrates Google's Watch Face Push so faces sync directly from the app instead of installing per-face from Play. Facer pricing: free faces, a-la-carte premium faces, subscription. — [Wareable, Facer platform update for Wear OS 6](https://www.wareable.com/android-wear/facer-watch-face-platform-update-wear-os-6) (secondary; surfaced via search summary, page not fetched by me)
- Pujie splits into two apps: one for Wear OS 6+ (WFF only) and one for Wear OS 4 and older (legacy). Android Authority reported WatchMaker would likely keep a single app and (citing an Italian report) had converted over 130,000 faces to WFF. — [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/after-facer-pujie-and-watchmaker-announce-wear-os-6-support-3581417/) (search summary; page returned 403 to my fetch)
- A third-party app-data site lists "Pujie Watch Faces - Wear OS 6" (com.pujie.watchfaces), last updated 2026-01-25. Marked low confidence by that site. — [marlvel.ai listing](https://marlvel.ai/api/llm/apps/personalization/com-pujie-watchfaces) (unverified)
- A third-party store mirror lists TIMEFLIK v10.4.0 (last updated 2026-07-14) saying "Galaxy Watch 7, 8 Series and all Wear OS 6+ devices have official support" and directing Wear OS 6 users to switch to the new version in settings. — [Cafe Bazaar mirror of TIMEFLIK](https://cafebazaar.ir/app/com.apposter.watchmaker?l=en) (third-party mirror; does not state it uses Watch Face Push explicitly)
- A March 2026 Wareable overview says platforms like Facer now support Watch Face Push. — [Wareable Wear OS 6 overview](https://www.wareable.com/android-wear/google-wear-os-6-new-features-which-watches-rollout) (secondary)
- Google's own non-marketplace example: Androidify (Dec 18, 2025). Phone app takes a selfie, builds an avatar with Gemini/Imagen, merges with a WFF template, assembles an APK on the phone with the Portable Asset Compiler Kit (Pack, run as a native library), validates it, and pushes it to the watch via MessageClient/ChannelClient + Watch Face Push. — [Android Developers Blog, Androidify with Watch Face Push](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2025/12/bringing-androidify-to-wear-os-with.html); [developer.android.com version](https://developer.android.com/blog/posts/bringing-androidify-to-wear-os-with-watch-face-push)
- The androidx 1.0.0 release note describes the library as designed for "cases where a user creates or edits a watch face on their phone and adds it to a connected watch." — [AndroidX release notes, wear-watchfacepush](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/androidx/releases/wear-watchfacepush)
- Pack library source: https://github.com/google/pack ; Androidify source: https://github.com/android/androidify (both linked from the Androidify post above).

### Inferences
- Google itself demonstrates the exact pattern "phone app generates a WFF APK locally, validates, pushes" (Androidify), and the 1.0.0 release notes explicitly name that use case, so the pattern is endorsed, not just tolerated.
- Real-world shipping marketplaces (Facer at minimum) are using a one-face-at-a-time model because of the single slot.

### Gaps
- I could not verify from a primary source (Play listing or vendor announcement) that WatchMaker, TIMEFLIK, Pujie or Recreative each ship Watch Face Push in production in 2026. Only Facer has first-party-ish evidence (its own forum) and press coverage. Recreative appears only in Google's partner/diagram caption.
- Whether Androidify is itself published on Google Play was not established in my sources (it is open source on GitHub).

## Exact API rules (slots, activation, naming, validator/token, signing, APK contents, eligibility, on-device generated faces)

### Takeaway
One slot on Wear OS 6; `setWatchFaceAsActive` can be used only once per app (permission denial also cannot be retried); package must be `<app>.watchfacepush.<name>`; APK has a strict whitelist and must carry a token from the official validator; faces are signed with a key different from the app key. No approval process for developers is described anywhere in the docs, and generating faces on-device is explicitly supported by the validator-on-Android library and by Google's Androidify sample.

### Cited Findings
- Latest androidx release: `androidx.wear.watchfacepush:watchfacepush` 1.0.0 stable, released 2026-04-08. History: 1.0.0-alpha01 (2025-06-18; previously `androidx.wear.watchface:watchface-push` 1.3.0-alpha07, April 2025), 1.0.0-beta01 (2025-12-03: minSdk lowered to 33, added `isSupported`, false below SDK 36), rc01 (2026-03-11), rc02 (2026-03-25, Guava CVE-2023-2976 fix). — [AndroidX release notes](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/androidx/releases/wear-watchfacepush)
- Validator is separate and still alpha: `com.google.android.wearable.watchface.validator:validator-push:1.0.0-alpha10` (also `-android`, and a CLI jar `validator-push-cli-1.0.0-alpha10.jar`). The library route needs an extra JitPack repo scoped to `com.github.xgouchet`. — [AndroidX release notes](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/androidx/releases/wear-watchfacepush); [Watch Face Push guide](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-push)
- Docs page "Watch Face Push" (also served at `/watch-face-push-policies`) last updated 2026-09-21 UTC. — [developer.android.com/watch-face-push-policies](https://developer.android.com/watch-face-push-policies)
- Slots: "The system sets a maximum number of slots that a marketplace can have; with Wear OS 6, the limit is 1." Do not persist slotIds; re-read via `listWatchFaces()` (returns installed list plus `remainingSlotCount`) before any slot operation. If no slot free, use `updateWatchFace(slotId, fd, token)` to replace. — [Wear OS app guide](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-push/wear-os-app); Androidify post (same behavior: add if free, else update) — [blog](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2025/12/bringing-androidify-to-wear-os-with.html)
- Activation: needs runtime permission `com.google.wear.permission.SET_PUSHED_WATCH_FACE_AS_ACTIVE`; "This permission has a maximum rejection count of 1: If the user denies the request, then the request cannot be made again" (user must go to settings). "The active watch face can be set by this means *only once*. Should the user move to a watch face from another developer, calling this API to set the active watch face back to your watch face throws an exception." After that, the phone app should guide the user to set the face manually (touch-and-hold). — [Wear OS app guide](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-push/wear-os-app); [Phone app guide](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-push/phone-app) (recommends the watch persist whether `setWatchFaceAsActive` has been called)
- Manifest permission `com.google.wear.permission.PUSH_WATCH_FACES` is "required to use the Watch Face Push API." — [Wear OS app guide](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-push/wear-os-app)
- API exception codes for activation include `ERROR_MAXIMUM_ATTEMPTS_REACHED` (note: "attempts", plural, vs docs text "only once"), `ERROR_MISSING_PERMISSION`, `ERROR_INVALID_SLOT_ID`, `ERROR_UNKNOWN`. — [androidx source mirror of reference docs](https://github.com/appdevforall/OfflineDocumentationTools) (secondary; official class reference page 404'd for me); API surface file: [androidx/androidx wear/watchfacepush api/current.txt](https://github.com/androidx/androidx/blob/androidx-main/wear/watchfacepush/watchfacepush/api/current.txt)
- Activation scenarios per Google: (a) marketplace already controls active face -> nothing needed; (b) permission granted -> call directly; (c) otherwise guide user through permission or manual long-press selection. — [Phone app guide](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-push/phone-app)
- Warning: do not call `isWatchFaceActive()` right after `updateWatchFace()`; the swap takes time. — [Wear OS app guide](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-push/wear-os-app)
- Package naming: `<app name>.watchfacepush.<watchface name>` where `<app name>` is the calling app's package; "The API rejects watch faces that don't conform." — [Watch Face Push guide](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-push)
- APK contents: only `/AndroidManifest.xml`, `/resources.arsc`, `/res/**`, `/META-INF/**`. Manifest tags limited to `<manifest>`, `<uses-feature>`, `<uses-sdk>`, `<application>`, `<property>`, `<meta-data>`. `minSdk >= 33`; `<application android:hasCode="false">`; one WFF face per APK. Google notes APKs with innocuous extra files may pass Play but fail Push validation. — [Watch Face Push guide](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-push)
- Validation: every face installed/updated must carry a validation token from the official, up-to-date validator; tokens do not expire even when the validator is superseded; unchanged APKs need no re-validation but periodic re-validation is recommended; "Unlike regular watch faces distributed through Google Play, the Marketplace app is responsible for verifying that each Watch Face Push watch face is well formed and performant." The validator checks the APK is a valid watch face plus some performance checks. — [Watch Face Push guide](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-push); [Androidify post](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2025/12/bringing-androidify-to-wear-os-with.html)
- Signing: "you must sign all your watch faces. Create a different key from the key you use with your main app and use the different key for all your watch faces." — [Watch Face Push guide](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-push)
- Default watch face: bundle `assets/default_watchface.apk` in the watch app plus manifest meta-data `com.google.android.wearable.marketplace.DEFAULT_WATCHFACE_VALIDATION_TOKEN`; this does not itself set it active; bundled defaults are not auto-updated on app update (listen for `MY_PACKAGE_REPLACED`). — [Wear OS app guide](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-push/wear-os-app)
- Architecture expected: cloud storage of prebuilt+validated APKs, phone app, watch app (bridge to the API), Data Layer for transport (ChannelClient/MessageClient/CapabilityClient). The watch app is mandatory because the API is only callable on the watch. — [Watch Face Push guide](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-push); [May 2025 blog](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2025/05/whats-new-in-watch-faces.html)
- Watch-side guidance: keep background work minimal, use `requiresCharging`, do not schedule jobs to check the active watch face, do not use notifications on the watch. — [Wear OS app guide](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-push/wear-os-app)
- Eligibility/approval: none of the Watch Face Push pages I read state any application, allowlist, or approval requirement for developers. The guide states the library "is meant for watch face developers who prefer to publish to their own marketplace stores"; for individual faces Google recommends Watch Face Designer / Watch Face Studio and Play. — [Phone app guide](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-push/phone-app); [Wear OS app guide](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-push/wear-os-app)
- On-device / phone-generated faces: not prohibited in any page I read. The Android-targeted validator artifact (`validator-push-android`) exists specifically so an app can generate tokens at runtime; Androidify does generation on the phone with Pack and validates on the phone before sending. The docs do not say whether Androidify's Gemini/Imagen step is server-side. — [Watch Face Push guide](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-push); [AndroidX release notes](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/androidx/releases/wear-watchfacepush); [Androidify post](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2025/12/bringing-androidify-to-wear-os-with.html)
- WFF itself is declarative XML with no executable code embedded in the face APK. — [Android Developers Blog, upcoming changes to Wear OS watch faces (Jun 2025)](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2025/06/upcoming-changes-to-wear-os-watch-faces.html)
- Issue tracker for the library: component 1235337. — [AndroidX release notes](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/androidx/releases/wear-watchfacepush)
- GitHub code search for `WatchFacePushManager`/`setWatchFaceAsActive` found only the AndroidX source/tests and doc mirrors; no third-party open-source marketplace repos surfaced. — GitHub search results (androidx/androidx).

### Inferences
- A phone app that builds a WFF APK from user input (Pack or hand-built resources.arsc/res), validates with `validator-push-android`, then pushes through a companion watch app is the Androidify pattern and fits the rules, provided the APK contents whitelist is respected (no code, no extra files).
- With a single slot, every new generated face replaces the previous one. Any "gallery" of saved faces must live on the phone and be re-pushed on selection.
- Because activation is one-shot per app and the permission cannot be re-prompted, onboarding UX should call `setWatchFaceAsActive` only for the very first install and then fall back to a manual "long-press to select" education screen; plan for the case where the user switched to another developer's face.
- The signing-key rule (separate face key, same for all faces) means the generator app on the phone must hold or derive that key; where the key lives (embedded in the app vs. server) is a security design decision the docs do not address.

### Gaps
- Official `WatchFacePushManager` reference page (and Kotlin reference) returned 404, so exact permission protection level (normal vs. signature/privileged) of `PUSH_WATCH_FACES` and `SET_PUSHED_WATCH_FACE_AS_ACTIVE` was not verified from a primary source. No evidence of approval is a negative finding, not proof.
- Docs say "only once" while the error constant is `ERROR_MAXIMUM_ATTEMPTS_REACHED`; whether failed calls consume the attempt is not documented.
- Behavior of `updateWatchFace` on the currently active face (does it stay active) is implied (Androidify relies on it) but I did not find an explicit statement.
- Whether validator-generated tokens are tied to the app package (CLI takes `--package_name`) and what happens if an unsigned/self-signed APK is used on retail devices was not independently tested.

## Google Play and platform policy: is a phone app that generates and pushes watch faces publishable?

### Takeaway
Nothing in the Watch Face Push docs, Play's Wear OS requirements, or the Play policies I could read prohibits a phone+watch app pairing that generates WFF faces and pushes them, and Google's own Androidify sample does it. The documented gates are standard: Wear OS app quality requirements, WFF-only (legacy faces dead since 2026-01-14), Play's Device and Network Abuse policy (no downloaded executable code; faces are code-free resources), UGC policy if faces are shared between users, and new-developer-account testing/verification. I found no dedicated "Watch Face Push policy" text beyond the technical guide. Direct confirmation of Play Console review treatment of PUSH_WATCH_FACES apps was not found.

### Cited Findings
- The page `developer.android.com/watch-face-push-policies` is, as served to my fetcher, the same technical guide ("Watch Face Push | Wear OS | Android Developers"); it has no policies section, no UGC/content rules, no eligibility rules. Last updated 2026-09-21 UTC. Google's Transparency Center Wear OS page links to it but gives no policy text and no dates. — [Watch Face Push page](https://developer.android.com/watch-face-push-policies); [Transparency Center, Wear OS](https://transparency.google/intl/en_us/our-policies/product-terms/wear-os/)
- Transparency Center: Wear OS developers "must also comply with Google Play's Developer Program Policies, guidelines, and terms." — [Transparency Center, Wear OS](https://transparency.google/intl/en_us/our-policies/product-terms/wear-os/)
- WFF-only migration: no new legacy (AndroidX/WSL) watch faces on Play since 2025-01-27; from 2026-01-14 users cannot install legacy faces from Play, developers cannot update them, and purchases/subscriptions on legacy faces stop renewing. — [Android Developers Blog, Jun 2025](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2025/06/upcoming-changes-to-wear-os-watch-faces.html)
- Wear OS app quality page (last updated 2026-08-06 UTC) does not mention Watch Face Push or marketplaces. Relevant dates: new apps must target API 34+ (from 2025-08-31); WFF required (Jan 2026); watch face icon requirement WO-G4 from 2026-07-15 (added to the page 2026-06-15); 64-bit support required by 2026-09-15. Watch face requirements include WO-P3 (users can install, set, personalize without crashing), WO-P7 (AOD <= 15% pixels), WO-P8 (memory limits 10 MB ambient / 100 MB interactive), WO-P10 (<= 8 complication slots), WO-G10 (<= 10 `<WatchFace>` elements in watch_face_shapes.xml), WO-G11 (XML <= 10 MB). — [Wear OS app quality](https://developer.android.com/docs/quality-guidelines/wear-app-quality)
- Google Play Device and Network Abuse policy: apps must not install other apps without explicit user consent; "an app may not download executable code (such as dex, JAR, .so files) from a source other than Google Play"; exception for code running in a VM or interpreter. Pushed WFF faces contain no code (`hasCode=false`), so this policy is not obviously triggered, but I found no official statement from Google confirming that interpretation. — [Google Play Developer Policy Center: Device and Network Abuse](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9888379)
- Google Play User Generated Content policy: apps hosting UGC (visible to at least a subset of other users) need terms acceptance before posting, defined prohibited content, ongoing moderation, and in-app report/block. Applies only if faces created by users are shared with other users; purely local, private face generation is not UGC under that definition (my reading). — [Play Console Help, UGC policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9876937) (via search summary; page not fully read by me)
- IP/impersonation: I did not retrieve the Play Impersonation / Intellectual Property policy text in this session (e.g., user-uploaded logos, brand faces); treat as unresearched.
- New personal developer accounts (created after 2023-11-13) must run a closed test with at least 12 testers opted in continuously for 14 days before applying for production; Production and Pre-registration stay locked until then; review usually takes seven days or less. Organization accounts are not covered by this requirement. — [Play Console Help: testing requirements for new personal accounts](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465). (Organization-exempt statement: secondary, [testerscommunity.com guide](https://www.testerscommunity.com/blog/google-play-closed-testing-requirements-2026))
- Registration fee: not re-verified in this session (the long-standing one-time USD 25 fee is from my background knowledge, unverified here).
- Android developer verification: reported rollout starting March 2026 for all developers, enforcement from September 2026 in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore, Thailand, then wider; Play developers are said to mostly satisfy it already via Play Console. Applies to sideloaded apps on certified devices. — [9to5Google, Aug 2025](https://9to5google.com/2025/08/25/android-apps-developer-verification/) and [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/?p=375237) (secondary; not read by me directly; unverified whether Wear OS is in scope)
- The Wear OS quality page requires Play Console self-tagging for watch faces (WO-G9) and specific listing screenshot/icon formats (WO-G4, G6); these apply to watch-face listings, and the quality page says apps with multiple watch faces or non-watch-face apps are exempt from WO-G4. A marketplace/generator app is not a watch face app in Play's sense. — [Wear OS app quality](https://developer.android.com/docs/quality-guidelines/wear-app-quality)
- Google states bundled Push faces do not get Play's per-face review: marketplace responsible for quality. Pushed faces are not Play-distributed packages, so Play does not scan the generated APKs. — [Watch Face Push guide](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/watch-face-push)

### Inferences
- The publishable shape is: a mobile app (phone, containing the generator) plus a Wear OS app (the bridge, with PUSH_WATCH_FACES) both on Play. Generated faces are not individually published to Play.
- Because the generated APK is not on Play, Play's "downloaded executable code" rule is the only policy where a reviewer might ask questions; the code-free WFF format and the validator whitelist (`hasCode=false`, no dex/so) are the argument for compliance.
- If the app only generates faces for the same user on-device, UGC moderation is not required; adding sharing/gallery/community features triggers it.
- Solo individual developer with an account created after 2023-11-13 faces the 12-tester/14-day closed test before production; an organization account avoids it.

### Gaps
- No Google page found that explicitly says "apps using Watch Face Push are/are not allowed on Play when faces are generated by the user"; also no Play Console declaration form for PUSH_WATCH_FACES was found (search returned none; weak negative evidence).
- Play's IP/impersonation and Spam/Minimum Functionality policy impact on a "face generator" was not researched.
- Current registration fee and exact developer-verification requirements for Wear OS apps were not verified against primary Google pages.
- Whether Play review has rejected or flagged any Watch Face Push app: no reports found.

### Gaps

## Known developer limitations and complaints / updates timeline

### Takeaway
Main documented limitations: one slot, one-shot activation, non-retriable permission, strict APK whitelist, validator still alpha, Bluetooth transfer size sensitivity, and the API existing only on Wear OS 6+. No 2026 policy change for Watch Face Push was found; the library went stable in April 2026.

### Inferences
- The main product-design constraint is the combination of 1 slot + one-shot activation; this shapes any "library of faces" UX (keep library on phone, re-push on tap).
- The validator being alpha-tagged while the runtime library is 1.0.0 stable is a maintenance risk: token generation behavior may change; tokens themselves never expire.
