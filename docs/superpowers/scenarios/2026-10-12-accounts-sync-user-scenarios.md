# Accounts and sync (optional Google sign-in, saved designs sync, signing-key backup): user scenarios and where each is tested

Index of what a person can do and see with the accounts feature of the phone app and which automated test protects it. Spec:
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-accounts-sync-design.md`. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-09-accounts-sync.md`. This is a coverage
catalogue, not a queue item. It continues `2026-10-10-onboarding-user-scenarios.md` (the first-run flow; its page 5, the backup page, is section I here),
`2026-10-09-app-shell-user-scenarios.md` (the Account destination around the section) and `2026-10-08-builder-poc-user-scenarios.md`. The things
nobody has tried on a real phone, a real Google account or a real Firebase project are listed at the end and in `docs/accounts/real-device-checklist.md`
(every line of it is NOT RUN).

Actors (the "Actor" column): **one phone** = a person with one phone who signs in (or never does); **new phone** = a person replacing a phone and
restoring on it; **two phones** = a person signed in on two phones at once; **forgot** = a person who forgot the recovery passphrase; **deleting** =
a person deleting the account; **never** = a person who never signs in (and a build without accounts); **setup** = a person on page 5 of the first-run
setup; **bad cloud** = a hostile or broken cloud (another user's data, the rules or App Check saying no, no network, a used-up quota).

Test layers (all under `phone/` unless noted): **JVM** = `./gradlew :phone:testDebugUnitTest`, in `src/test/kotlin/com/seyedjafari/analogplus/phone/`
(`accounts/`, `accounts/vault/`, `ui/account/`; fakes in `src/sharedTest`); **UI** = Compose tests on the phone emulator over the in-memory fakes of the
account manager, the cloud and the sync (`AccountUiFixture`, no Firebase), in `src/androidTest/kotlin/com/seyedjafari/analogplus/phone/` (`accounts/`
is the sub-folder of the same tree; a few are in the folder itself: `AccountFlowTest`, `DeepLinkTest`, `LibraryAccountTest`, `PromptRecreationTest`,
`Onboarding*`), run with `scripts/with_phone_emulator_lock.sh ./gradlew :phone:connectedDebugAndroidTest`
(`-Pandroid.testInstrumentationRunnerArguments.class=a.B,c.D` picks classes; those in `accounts/` also run in the accounts script below); **EMU** = the instrumented tests that `scripts/accounts_e2e.sh` runs (package `accounts`; most need the **Firebase Emulator Suite**, Auth 9099 and
Firestore 8080 with `firebase/firestore.rules` loaded, a few such as `PhotoDownscalerTest` and `KeyVaultProviderTest` only need the phone emulator): the real `FirebaseAccountManager`, `FirestoreRemoteStore`, `AccountsFactory`, `SyncEngine`, `BackupService`
(production key-derivation cost) and `AccountDeletion`, and in `AccountsEndToEndTest` also the real Account card, its dialogs, the offer dialog and
the Library screen; run with `ANDROID_SERIAL=emulator-5562 scripts/accounts_e2e.sh [--class a.B,c.D]`, which starts and stops the Firebase emulators and
fails (does not skip) when they are not reachable; **RULES** = `cd firebase && npm test` (`firebase/tests/rules.test.js`, Firestore rules in the
emulator, also in the CI workflow). Test names below are `File.testName`; a name starting with a dot is another test of the file before it; a file name
alone means every test in it.

How the EMU tests drive the app: `AccountsEndToEndTest` builds each "phone" with its own `Store`, key file, sync preferences and controller from the real
`AccountsFactory`, shows the real Account card (`AccountSection`, with `AccountOfferHost` beside it as the shell hosts it) or the real Library screen over
that phone's store in a pinned window, and presses the same buttons a person presses (sign in, back up, restore, forgot, delete, Keep my account,
Use theirs, Save, Load, Delete); every wait is for a visible text or tag or for a bounded poll of the emulator through its REST endpoint, never a fixed
sleep (the few negative checks, "nothing went up", wait a fixed 1.5 s, each marked in its test). The sign-in sandbox is the Auth emulator, which accepts a JSON "ID token" for a
Google token (`EmulatorSetup.tokenSource`). **The two boundaries that are replaced**: the Google chooser (the emulator's stand-in answers, or answers
"closed" when a test says the person closed it) and the second device: the "phones" of one test live in ONE process, so they share one `FirebaseAuth`
and one `FirebaseFirestore` client; a sign-out on one signs the other out, which is why the kill-switch tests give the second phone a `FakeAccountManager`
(the real engine, cloud and controller, a fake sign-in). Each test empties the emulators before and after and uses its own e-mail address.

## Not covered, and why (out of scope: the owner knows these are unverified)

- **Real Google sign-in and Credential Manager.** The emulator tests sign in through the Auth emulator, which accepts a JSON "ID token"; the real chooser, the
  Play-services sheet on Android 13 and lower, the system sheet on 14 and newer, the SHA-1 registration and Google's own account selection were never run.
  `docs/accounts/real-device-checklist.md` A1, B1 to B4.
- **Firebase App Check and Play Integrity.** Not exercised (needs a real project with enforcement on): a refused call is tested as the rules' answer
  (H4), not as App Check's. Checklist F1 to F3.
- **A real Firebase project and the deployed rules.** Everything ran against the Firebase Emulator Suite; deployed rules are part of the kill switch
  (owner-prerequisites item 5). Quota and billing limits are not enforced by the emulator (H5).
- **A real paired phone and watch, the Wear Data Layer, and the Galaxy Watch (SM-L315F) reset.** Emulators cannot be paired without a Google sign-in. What
  starting over without the passphrase does on the watch (E12, E14) is only what the Wear OS emulator spike saw; the screen is pinned to that and promises
  no more. Checklist E1, E2, D3, D5.
- **Two real devices.** The "phones" of one EMU test share one process, one `FirebaseAuth` and one `FirebaseFirestore` client: a sign-in or sign-out on one
  is seen by the other, so the tests cannot hold two different Firebase sessions at once. The kill-switch tests therefore give the second phone a fake
  sign-in (the real engine, cloud and controller). What it would take to close this: a second `FirebaseApp` with its own `FirebaseAuth` and
  `FirebaseFirestore` inside `AccountsFactory` (an app change), or a second emulator or device running the same test with the same e-mail.
- **A real process kill.** A test cannot kill its own process. The cut-off deletion cancels the controller's scope the moment the pending mark is written and
  builds a new controller over the same storage (F6, G2); what a real kill adds is unverified (checklist C2).
- **A phone that is offline, or that has not run for an hour, through a real deletion** (G5, the token's life, the sign-out by Google's own service without the
  notice), **real airplane mode and a slow link** (checklist A7, G2, G12, G16): the offline paths are tested by switching the Firestore client's network off,
  by timeouts and by faults injected in the fakes.
- **A release build without `google-services.json`.** The instrumented tests run the debug build, which always has accounts (emulator mode); "accounts off" is
  tested through the unavailable controller and the `NOT_CONFIGURED` availability (A3 to A5), not through a built release APK. What it would take: a
  `connectedReleaseAndroidTest` run with no `google-services.json` and the screens driven by the real `AccountsFactory`.
- **TalkBack.** Semantics are asserted (live regions, names, headings, announcements), not the spoken output or the focus order. Checklist G15.
- **Dark theme beyond one button and the pictures** (J3). The card, the dialogs and the page are drawn in the dark theme for a look by eye only.
- **Many saved designs on a slow link, traffic over a minute of editing, an almost full disk, a phone with the wrong clock, accented passphrases on a real
  phone** (checklist G2, G3, G11, G16, G10): the logic is tested (C11, E2, E7, E18); the real conditions are not.
- **The accepted limits of the spec** (D13, G9, H9): a late-landing design write, the token living up to an hour, a backup write without a time limit, tombstones that
  stay until the account is deleted.
